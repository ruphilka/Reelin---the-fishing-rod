# Cat Fishing Rod Toy - Ronin Toys Edition

A modified version of the popular 3D printed cat fishing rod toy featuring the Ronin Toys logo and enhanced functionality for 8mm wooden dowels with an improved lure system.

![Cat Fishing Rod Toy](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-brightgreen) ![3D Print](https://img.shields.io/badge/3D%20Print-Ready-blue) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA-orange)

## Overview

This project is based on the excellent [Cat Fishing Rod Toy - Unglued Remix](https://www.printables.com/model/409481-cat-fishing-rod-toy-unglued-remix) but includes several key modifications to align with [Ronin Toys](https://ronintoys.com/) design philosophy and improve functionality:

- **Ronin Toys branding** integrated into the design
- **8mm wooden dowel support** for a more natural, sustainable rod feel
- **Enhanced lure system** using heat shrink tubing and spade connectors for secure feather attachment
- Maintains the original's **glue-free assembly** design

## Features

### Original Design Elements (Preserved)
- Functional ratcheting reel with printed spring
- Satisfying clicking sound during line retrieval
- Glue-free assembly using dovetailed and threaded parts
- Easy disassembly for maintenance and part replacement
- Support-free printing orientation

### Ronin Toys Modifications
- **Ronin Toys Logo**: Integrated branding elements that reflect the cooperative chaos philosophy
- **8mm Wooden Dowel Compatibility**: Modified pole guides and mounts to accommodate standard 8mm wooden dowels instead of fiberglass rods
- **Professional Lure System**: Redesigned lure attachment using heat shrink tubing and crimp-on spade connectors for secure feather mounting
- **Enhanced Durability**: Optimized for the rigorous testing standards of our feline research team

## Bill of Materials

### 3D Printed Parts
- 1x WinderBase (with Ronin Toys branding)
- 1x PoleReelMount (modified for 8mm dowel)
- 1x Grip
- 1x InnerReel
- 1x InnerReelTeeth
- 1x OuterReel
- 1x ReelCap
- 1x ReelHandle (choose HeatSet or NoHeatSet version)
- 1x Release
- 1x ReleaseCover
- 1x ReelGuide
- Multiple PoleGuides (quantity depends on dowel length, modified for 8mm)
- 1x PoleGuideCap (modified for 8mm dowel)

### Hardware & Materials
- **8mm wooden dowel** - 30cm or larger (36" length recommended)
- **M3x10 button head hex screw** - 1pc
- **M3xD5xL4 brass heat-set insert** - 1pc (if using HeatSet version)
- **Paracord or string** - approximately 4 meters
- **Heat shrink tubing** - small pieces for lure assembly
- **Spade connector** - small gauge for feather attachment
- **Cat feather toy** or similar lure
- **Lobster clasp** (optional, for easy lure swapping)

### Tools Required
- 3D printer
- Soldering iron (for heat-set insert)
- Crimping tool (for spade connector)
- Heat gun or lighter (for heat shrink tubing)

## Print Settings

### Recommended Settings
- **Layer Height**: 0.2mm
- **Infill**: 40% (can experiment with other densities)
- **Supports**: None required - all parts designed for support-free printing
- **Orientation**: Parts are pre-oriented for optimal printing

### Filament Recommendations
- **PLA+**: Recommended for all parts except the ratchet spring
- **PETG**: Required for the ratchet spring component only (better flexibility and durability)

**Pro Tip**: Consider printing the reel components in different colors for a striking visual effect, following Ronin Toys' "Perfect Chaos™" aesthetic.

## Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Base Assembly
1. Slide the PoleReelMount into the dovetail socket on the reverse side of the WinderBase
2. Thread the Grip onto the PoleReelMount, locking it into place
3. Insert the Release through the small slot in the WinderBase, ensuring free movement

### Step 2: Release Mechanism
1. Carefully break free the thin spring on the ReleaseCover
2. Install onto the WinderBase with the round pin through the small hole in the Release
3. Test that the Release springs back when pressed and released

### Step 3: Reel Handle
**For HeatSet Version:**
1. Use a soldering iron to carefully melt the brass heat-set insert into the ReelHandle
2. Attach the ReelHandle to the OuterReel using the M3 screw

**For NoHeatSet Version:**
1. Thread the M3 screw directly into the plastic (avoid over-tightening)

### Step 4: Reel Assembly
1. Tie one end of your cord through the two small holes on the inside of the OuterReel
2. Thread the InnerReel onto the InnerReelTeeth
3. Thread the OuterReel onto the InnerReelTeeth
4. Insert the complete reel assembly into the WinderBase
5. Thread the ReelCap into the WinderBase to secure the assembly

### Step 5: Rod and Line Setup
1. Slide the ReelGuide over the end of the PoleReelMount
2. Insert the 8mm wooden dowel into the PoleReelMount
3. Install PoleGuides along the dowel (should fit tightly)
4. Install the PoleGuideCap on the end of the dowel
5. Feed the cord through all PoleGuides

### Step 6: Enhanced Lure Assembly
1. Strip a small section of the cord end
2. Crimp the spade connector onto the stripped cord
3. Slide heat shrink tubing over the connection
4. Apply heat to shrink the tubing for a weatherproof seal
5. Attach your feather or other lure to the spade connector
6. Optional: Add a lobster clasp for easy lure swapping

## Maintenance

### Regular Care
- Check reel mechanism periodically for smooth operation
- Replace cord when frayed or worn
- Clean 3D printed parts with mild soap and water
- Inspect lure attachment for security

### Part Replacement
The glue-free design allows easy replacement of worn components:
- Individual reel components can be reprinted and swapped
- Cord can be replaced without disassembly
- Lure system components are field-replaceable

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
**Reel not clicking**: Check that the Release mechanism moves freely and the spring is properly seated

**Line tangles**: Ensure PoleGuides are properly aligned and cord moves smoothly through all guides

**Loose dowel fit**: Print replacement PoleGuides with slightly smaller inner diameter

**Lure detachment**: Verify spade connector is properly crimped and heat shrink is secure
